Partner With Us

At Inaya, we blend the discipline of institutional capital advisory with the instincts of modern origination. Our clients are fund managers, sponsors, founders, and private wealth principals. We pride ourselves on being operators first, advisors second, bringing a practical, ownership-driven view to every mandate. The firm is led by people who have built and run businesses themselves, and each mandate is approached with the practical view of an owner.

With deep transactional experience across capital formation, M&A, and private wealth, we bring more than advice. The firm acts on fund raises, real estate and infrastructure projects, growth capital and private wealth introductions, and every engagement is qualified before it begins and built to close.

Capital Markets

When you are raising, the difficulty is rarely the merit of the business. It is reaching the allocators whose mandate actually fits yours, in the order that builds momentum rather than spends it, and arriving in front of them already understood, framed as the opportunity it is rather than one more deck in the pile.

We identify the allocators suited to each mandate and prepare them before the first conversation, so the raise reaches them already positioned to be understood on its own terms. We run that process from positioning through to close.

Mergers & Acquisitions

Not every situation calls for capital. A founder may be weighing a full exit, a family a succession, a principal the sale of a minority stake, each resting on judgment well before it rests on price. We advise them through the whole of it, from how it is positioned and valued to how it is structured and timed.

We take one situation at a time and confirm fit on both sides before any conversation begins, so the counterparty in the room is one with genuine reason to be there.

Your raise goes to our network first.

A conversation with our Client Advisor about what you are raising and the timeline you are working to. You come away with a direct first read on how the raise is likely to land with allocators.

30 Minutes

Senior Review

Our senior team works through your materials and terms. Within 48 to 72 hours you have a clear answer on whether we would take the mandate, and what we would change before any investor sees it.

48–72 Hours

Positioning

We shape the materials and the terms around the questions an investment committee asks before it commits, so your mandate is defensible on every point before it reaches a single allocator.

To Close
