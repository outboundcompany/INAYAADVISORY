Partner With Us

Inaya Advisory works with fund managers, sponsors, founders and private wealth principals on capital raising, M&A and private market transactions. The firm brings an operator's view to every mandate: how the business runs, how the terms will read to an investor, and what it takes to reach a close.

Mandates include fund raises, real estate and infrastructure projects, growth capital, sale processes and private wealth introductions. Each is reviewed before it is accepted, and each is run by senior people from the first assessment to close.

Capital Markets

We run capital raises for funds, sponsors and companies: positioning, materials, the investor list, introductions, meetings and diligence, through to close.

Each raise goes first to the investors in our network whose mandate fits it, in an order set to build momentum across the process.

Mergers & Acquisitions

We advise founders, families and principals on full exits, successions and minority stake sales, from positioning and valuation through structure and timing.

We take one situation at a time, and qualify counterparties on both sides before any introduction is made.
