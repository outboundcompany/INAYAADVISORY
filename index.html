Equity Capital Raising and Advisory for Private Markets.

We advise fund managers, sponsors and founders raising equity from US$100 million to several billion, and owners weighing a sale. Every mandate is reviewed by our senior team before we accept it, and the materials and terms are prepared before any investor sees the raise.

The Firm

Senior judgment on every mandate.

Partner With Us

Inaya Advisory advises fund managers, sponsors, founders and private wealth principals. Each mandate is reviewed by our senior team before we accept it, and the people who review it are the people who run it, from the first read of the materials to the final close and into investor relations.

We work on equity only, across fund commitments, growth equity, real assets equity and the sale of a business. Debt, credit and lending sit outside our practice. Every engagement is qualified before it begins, and its terms are confirmed in writing before any work starts.

Global Access

Our investor relationships are direct, and every introduction is made personally.

We hold standing relationships with pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, endowments, family offices and private wealth principals in North America, Europe and the Gulf. A raise goes to our network first, in a set order. Introductions are made personally, and only to investors whose mandate fits, so an allocator who hears from us knows the raise has been reviewed.

Capital Raising Mandates

When a manager is raising, the difficulty is rarely the merit of the fund or the business. It is reaching the investors whose mandate fits, in an order that builds commitments, with materials an investment committee can use.

We take the raise to our network first, in a set order, prepare every investor meeting with the client and stay in the conversations through diligence and terms to the final close.

Investor Relations

The investors who commit to a raise are the first people the next raise will need. Those relationships are kept working after the close and between funds.

We keep each investor informed on a schedule agreed with the client, with updates written for the people who report to a committee. Investor relations is offered after a mandate or on its own.

Targeted Introductions

Some raises are prepared and already in market, and need one investor, or a small number of them, whose mandate fits.

We confirm the fit on both sides before either party hears the other's name. Each introduction is made personally, and our name goes with every one.

Mergers & Acquisitions

A founder may be weighing a full exit, a family a succession, a principal the sale of a minority stake. We advise on whether and when to sell, how the business is prepared and presented, and how the sale is structured and timed.

We confirm what the owner wants before any work on value begins, and we confirm fit on both sides before any conversation with a counterparty.

Positioning & Materials

Most of the work that decides a raise happens before any investor sees it. A raise gets one first reading.

We prepare the deck, the track record, the data room and the terms around the questions an investment committee asks, so the answers are on the page before the first meeting.

Investment Committee Review

Before any investor sees a raise, our senior team reviews it the way an investment committee will. David Hunter, our Senior Advisor, was Executive Director and Chief Investment Officer of the North Dakota Retirement and Investment Office and of the US$40 billion Texas Municipal Retirement System.

We find what an allocator will challenge, from the track record and the terms to the team, the pipeline and the data room, and we fix it before diligence begins, so the mandate holds up when it is tested.
