The Firm

Inaya Advisory

Inaya Advisory is an independent capital raising firm. We raise equity for fund managers, sponsors and founders in private markets.

Firm facts

  • Inaya Advisory is the trading name of Inaya LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company formed on 3 June 2026 under filing ID 2026-001995889.
  • Registered office: 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States.
  • Every mandate is governed by a written engagement letter, agreed before work begins. It names who works on your raise.

What we do, and what we don't

We raise equity for fund commitments, deal-by-deal and co-investment vehicles, and private companies, from US$50 million.

We don't arrange debt, credit or lending, or work with retail investors.

References to investor relationships describe relationships our team has built over their careers.

Whether capital is committed is decided by investors after their own diligence. We do not promise an outcome.
