Our principles

A principle that bends for a large mandate is a preference. Ours hold on every one.

  1. 01

    Equity, and the sale of a business

    Fund commitments, growth equity, real assets equity and the sale of a business. Debt, credit and lending sit outside our practice.

  2. 02

    Prepared before it is seen

    The materials and terms answer what an investment committee asks before any investor reads them. A raise gets one first reading.

  3. 03

    Introductions carry our name

    We introduce personally and only to investors whose mandate fits. An allocator who reads our note knows the raise has been reviewed.

  4. 04

    A decision with reasons

    After senior review you have our answer. When it is no, we say so and give the reasons.

  5. 05

    The investor's time is protected

    A raise reaches an allocator only when it fits its mandate, so every introduction we make is one it expects to read.

  6. 06

    Senior people, start to finish

    The people who accept a mandate are the people who run it, from the first conversation to the final close.

Mandate selection

We choose our mandates. The questions a committee will ask are asked first by us.

  1. 01

    First conversation. With Abbas Chothia, our Client Advisor, in confidence, on the raise, the timeline and the constraints.

  2. 02

    Senior review. David Hunter and Anas Ansar test the raise against the questions a committee will ask. Our answer follows, with reasons.

  3. 03

    Diligence. Full diligence begins once fit and timing are agreed on both sides, and scope and terms are confirmed in writing.

Mandates we accept generally run from US$100 million to several billion. Live mandates are never named; asked who we work with, we give sector and size only.

01What we look at

Team and track record

Who makes the decisions, and can results be attributed to them? How long the team has worked together, and which results it made.

02What we look at

Strategy and pipeline

Why this strategy, and where will the capital go? Where the strategy sits in its market and what the first part of the fund will finance.

03What we look at

Terms and alignment

Do the terms hold up in front of a committee? Management terms, the manager's own commitment and the governance an allocator will test.

04What we look at

Operations and reporting

Can the firm report the way institutions expect? Administration, valuation and reporting, and the people behind them.

Our clients

Our standards

What every client and every investor can expect from us.

  1. 01

    Confidentiality

    We never name live mandates. Asked who we work with, we give sector and size only, and client material stays with us.

  2. 02

    Engagements in writing

    We act only for the client that engages us, and the scope and terms are confirmed in writing before any work begins.

  3. 03

    Decisions with reasons

    Our answer follows a senior review of the raise. When the answer is no, we say so plainly and we give the reasons.

  4. 04

    What we publish

    Every figure on this website carries a public source, so that any reader, client or investor can check it for themselves.
