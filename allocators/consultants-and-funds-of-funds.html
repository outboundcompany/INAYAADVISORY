Consultants & Funds of Funds

Investment consultants advise institutions on which managers to back. Funds of funds and outsourced chief investment officers invest on their behalf. Together they shape which managers many institutions ever see.

The gatekeepers

For many institutions, a manager is considered only after a consultant has reviewed it.

Behind every consultant is a list of clients, from pension plans for public workers to endowments and foundations. Its research is how many of them first learn about a manager, so its review is detailed and documented.

Process and repeatability

A process that is written down, applied consistently and owned by more than one person.

Operations and attribution

Valuation, controls and service providers, and a track record shown deal by deal with the role of each person.

Terms against peers

Fund terms and governance measured against a peer group the consultant already covers, with any difference explained before it is asked.
