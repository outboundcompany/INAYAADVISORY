Open private raises: $129.8 billion still unraised.

Inaya Advisory · November 2026

Across Form D filings made in the twelve months to July 2026, 951 private offerings of $50 million or more remain open. Together they sought $149.3 billion. On the most recent filings, $129.8 billion of that is still to be raised.

How much has been sold

Most of these raises have made little progress on paper. 567 of the 951 reported nothing sold on their latest filing. A further 253 had sold less than a quarter of their target. Only 46 had sold more than half.

Amounts are as reported on each issuer's most recent Form D. Issuers are not required to amend a Form D for every sale, so reported figures can lag what has been raised.

Our view

The filings do not say why any single raise has not closed. In our experience, the raises that do close tend to have four things in place.

01

Materials that answer the committee

An investment committee comes to a raise with a familiar set of questions. Materials that answer them before the first meeting allow that meeting to be spent on the decision rather than on the basics.

02

A considered sequence

The order in which allocators see a raise shapes how the market reads it. A raise shown to everyone at once reads differently from one introduced in a deliberate order, to the right allocators first.

03

An introduction from someone known

Allocators give their time to people they know. An introduction from someone the allocator already knows is read and answered in a way that an unsolicited approach rarely is.

04

Follow-through to terms

Interest is not a commitment. Diligence requests, follow-up questions and the commitment conversations need to be carried through, without gaps, until terms are agreed.
