AE Industrial closes an $850 million continuation vehicle for Blue Raven.

November 2, 20263 min read

In the Market

November 2, 2026

3 min read

LinkedInEmail

AE Industrial Partners closed AE Industrial Partners BR Fund, a single-asset continuation vehicle with approximately $850 million of commitments, in connection with the recapitalisation of its portfolio company Blue Raven. Lexington Partners led the vehicle, and several other institutional investors took part.

Kirkland & Ellis, which advised AE Industrial, said Blue Raven management and certain existing shareholders rolled significant proceeds into the transaction. Simpson Thacher, which advised Lexington, described Blue Raven as a technology-enabled distribution platform serving the global defence market.

For a raiseWhat it means for a raise

A continuation vehicle lets a manager keep a strong asset for longer and gives existing investors the choice to sell or roll. Investors who sell receive cash they can commit to new funds.

For a manager preparing its next fund, a well-run continuation vehicle can return capital to its investors before the new fund is in market. Allocators will still ask how the price was set and how conflicts were managed.
