Advent closes its eleventh flagship buyout fund at close to US$27 billion.

October 27, 20263 min read

In the Market

October 27, 2026

3 min read

Advent International said it raised US$30 billion across its fund family, including close to US$27 billion for GPE XI, above a US$26 billion target, and over US$3 billion for a new mid-market strategy.

The firm said GPE XI is the largest buyout fund to finish raising so far in 2026, and Private Equity International ranks it third among private equity funds by PEI Group data. Commitments came from pensions, sovereign wealth funds, endowments, family offices and other investors.

For a raiseWhat it means for a raise

Advent paired the close with about US$13 billion of 2026 realisations, and the firm itself called this one of the most demanding fundraising environments.

Allocators are committing to managers that have returned cash. A manager in market should put its realised record at the front of the case.
