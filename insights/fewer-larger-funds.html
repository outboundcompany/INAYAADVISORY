Fewer funds are closing, and the largest take a bigger share.

October 29, 20263 min read

In the Market

October 29, 2026

3 min read

Book a ConsultationMore in In the Market
Share
LinkedInEmail

PitchBook's Q3 2026 First Look shows $368.1 billion of global private equity capital raised year to date, pacing up 3.1%, while the number of funds closed, 523, is pacing down 30.5%. In the US, capital raised was $222 billion, pacing down 10.3%.

Separately, PEI Group data shows that mega-funds closing in the first three quarters of 2026 gathered $169.5bn, or 24% of all capital raised. The two data providers count differently, and their figures should be read separately.

For a raiseWhat it means for a raise

Total capital raised is holding up, and it is going to fewer managers.

A first-time or mid-sized fund should plan for a longer process, a deeper list of allocators and a clear reason why its strategy differs from a large platform.
