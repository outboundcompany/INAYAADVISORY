01 Capital Raising

A full mandate runs a raise from senior review to the last side letter.

One team takes the mandate and stays on it until the capital is committed. The raise goes first to the over 200 investor relationships we hold across pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, endowments, family offices and private wealth in North America, Europe and the Gulf, in the order most likely to bring early commitments.

We prepare every investor meeting with you and attend those that decide the outcome. Diligence is answered as it arrives, through final terms to close.

01Before outreach

Positioning and Materials

How the raise is positioned against its market and the allocators it is meant for, then the thesis, the deck, the track record, the data room and the terms to carry it through committee.

02Before the first meeting

Investment Committee Review

Our senior team reads the raise the way an investment committee will, sets out in writing each point it is likely to challenge and changes the raise until each point has an answer. Also offered on its own.

03In market

Targeted Introductions

A personal introduction to a specific investor whose mandate fits, for a raise that is already prepared. We confirm the fit on both sides before either party hears the other's name.

04After the close

Investor Relations

We keep your investors informed on a schedule agreed with you, answer what they ask between funds and prepare the next raise with those most likely to commit again. Also offered on its own.

03 Proprietary Origination

We find and approach the other side of a mandate on our client's behalf.

Origination runs inside a capital raising or sale mandate, or on its own where a client needs the counterparties found.

  1. 01

    Investors, for a raise

    The investors whose mandate, ticket size and timing fit the raise, including those outside our existing network, approached on the client's behalf.

  2. 02

    Buyers, for a sale

    The acquirers and owners most likely to value the business, approached in confidence and in a set order.

  3. 03

    Targets, for an acquisition

    For a sponsor or a company buying, the businesses that fit the plan, with the first conversation opened with their owners.

  4. 04

    The approach

    Each approach is written for the person who receives it and made in our name. Fit is confirmed on both sides before names are shared.

Our method

Relationships open the door. Our research decides which doors, and when.

Our network is where a raise starts. Alongside it, we use public data to decide which investors to approach, in what order and at what moment, and we show the client every step.

Names are shared once an agreement with a non-circumvention clause is signed.

01Built from public data

Investor match

We read pension board minutes, foundation and endowment filings, SEC filings, family office hires and allocation announcements, and match what we find to the fund's strategy, size and timing.

02Before the call

A first look

Before the first call, a written read of which types of investor fit the raise, the ticket sizes they write and the order in which we would approach them.

03When a window opens

Live signals

We approach an investor when a new mandate, a hire or a board approval opens a window for the strategy, so the raise arrives when the investor is ready to look at it.

04After signing

A live dashboard

Clients see every approach on a live dashboard: who was approached, the response, the next step and its date.

How we work

A raise runs in a set order, and each stage has an owner.

Three conversations come before any engagement. Nothing reaches an investor until the raise is ready for a committee, and scope and terms are confirmed in writing before any work begins.

Where a raise calls for investors beyond our network, we select them for the mandate and write each approach for the investor who will receive it.
