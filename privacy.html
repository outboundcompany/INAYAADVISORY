Who we are

inayaadvisory.com is operated by Inaya LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company trading as Inaya Advisory, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, United States. Questions about this policy go to capital@inayaadvisory.com.

No website tracking

This site uses no analytics, no advertising tags and no tracking cookies. We do not record which pages you read. The booking tool may set only what it needs to work.

What we collect

  • When you book a conversation. Booking runs through Cal.com. We receive what you enter: your name, firm, business email, your role, what you are raising for, its size and your timeline.
  • When you email us. Your message and anything you attach.
  • When we speak. Calls with us are recorded and transcribed with Fathom, so we can prepare the senior review and keep an accurate record. We tell you at the start of the call. If you would rather not be recorded, say so and we will not record.
  • Business contact details. For our own business development, we use professional contact details from public sources, such as a name, role, firm and business email.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and run the conversations you ask for.
  • To prepare the senior review and proposed approach.
  • To run a mandate once an engagement letter is signed.
  • To keep the records our broker-dealer and the law require.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who sees it

  • Our team, on a need-to-know basis.
  • The service providers that run our scheduling (Cal.com), call recording (Fathom) and email, only to provide those services.
  • Finalis Securities LLC, our broker-dealer, which supervises our securities activity and keeps required records.
  • Our professional advisors, and anyone the law or a regulator requires us to share it with.

How long we keep it

As long as we need it for the purposes above. Some communications must be kept for set periods under broker-dealer record-keeping rules, even after a conversation or mandate ends.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you, by emailing capital@inayaadvisory.com. We reply within 30 days. Where the law requires us to keep a record, we will tell you.

Where it is stored

Our service providers may store information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date.
