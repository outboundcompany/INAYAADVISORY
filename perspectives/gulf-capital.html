Gulf Capital in Private Markets

Sovereign funds and family offices in the Gulf are among the most active investors in private markets anywhere, and much of their capital is invested abroad.

Where it goes

Recent deployment has focused on technology and infrastructure.

Mubadala led regional sovereign deployment in the first nine months of 2026 with US$26.2 billion, followed by PIF at US$14 billion, ADIA at US$12.2 billion, L'imad at US$10.8 billion and QIA at US$10.3 billion.

Source: Global SWF data reported by Khaleej Times, October 1, 2026. Inaya Advisory is not affiliated with any institution named.

Gulf allocators

How sovereign funds and family offices in the region engage with managers.

Large investors with long horizons

The region's sovereign funds invest directly and through funds, with teams organised by asset class. Many ask managers about co-investment alongside a fund commitment, and about the funds that will follow this one.

Learn more about sovereign wealth funds

Family capital, often from operating businesses

Many family offices in the Gulf manage wealth built in family businesses. They compare a fund commitment with direct deals they already see, and they give their time to people they know.

Learn more about family offices

Relationships before commitments

Gulf allocators commit to foreign managers they have come to know, often over several meetings in person. A manager should expect to present the strategy more than once, to more than one team.
