Accessibility

We want inayaadvisory.com to be usable by as many people as possible, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, screen magnification or voice control.

Last reviewed November 1, 2026

On this page

  1. Our approach
  2. How the site is built
  3. Known limits
  4. Telling us about a problem

Our approach

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA when we build and change this site. This is a statement of intent. We have not had the site audited or certified against the guidelines.

How the site is built

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, and the element in focus is outlined.
  • The main menu opens with the keyboard and closes with the Escape key. A skip link at the top of each page moves straight to the content.
  • Headings and page regions mark the structure of each page for assistive technology.
  • Images that carry information have a text alternative. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Pages reflow on small screens and when text is enlarged.
  • Animation is reduced or removed when your device is set to reduce motion.

Known limits

Some documents, such as the PDF brochures in our Education section, may not work fully with every assistive technology. If you need a document in another format, email us and we will send it in a format that suits you.

The Mandate Consultation page uses a scheduling service provided by another company to show available times. If you cannot book through the page, email us and we will arrange a time with you.

Telling us about a problem

If any part of this site is hard to use, email capital@inayaadvisory.com with the address of the page and a short description of the problem. We will look into it and reply.
