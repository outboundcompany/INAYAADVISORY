How we hold a mandate

Three things every mandate gets from us.

  1. 01

    Market terms

    Each mandate is measured against current terms and against comparable raises before it reaches an investor.

  2. 02

    Execution

    We carry the process through diligence and the closing mechanics, and we stay on it until the capital is committed.

  3. 03

    Discretion

    Client names and investor identities are protected, and nothing about a raise is circulated in the market.

How names are shared

Names are shared once an agreement with a non-circumvention clause is signed.

Live mandates are never named. Asked who we work with, we give sector and size only, and this list follows the same rule.
