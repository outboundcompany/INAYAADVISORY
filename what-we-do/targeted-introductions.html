Targeted Introductions

For a raise that is prepared and already running, we introduce one investor, or a small number of them, whose mandate fits. Each introduction is made personally, and only after both sides have confirmed the fit.

Who we know

Every introduction we make comes from a relationship we already hold.

01

Standing relationships

We hold over 200 investor relationships, among pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, endowments, family offices and private wealth in North America, Europe and the Gulf.

02

A relationship for every mandate

For every kind of mandate we take, we hold at least one standing investor relationship. An introduction starts with an investor who already knows us.

03

Gulf capital

Sovereign funds in the Middle East and North Africa deployed $102 billion in the first nine months of 2026, and the United States received 45% of the value invested.

Source for Gulf figures: Global SWF 2026 Mena Playbook, via Khaleej Times, October 1, 2026, as published in Gulf sovereign capital.

The introduction

We introduce a raise only when each side has a reason to take the meeting, and both have told us so.

Fit confirmed on both sides

We test the raise against the investor's mandate, its current allocation and its timing, then confirm with you and with the investor before either side hears the other's name.

A note written for that investor

Each introduction carries a short note on why this raise suits this investor, with the materials it will want to read first already prepared.

After the first meeting

We stay with the relationship through the first meeting and any diligence that follows. If the raise needs more than an introduction, we say so and set out what a full mandate adds.

When managers come to us

An introduction is asked for when a raise needs one investor it has not yet reached.

01

If you need an anchor investor, come to us.

The first commitment decides how every investor after it reads the raise. We take the raise to the investors most likely to anchor it, in a set order, before the market has formed a view.

02

If you need investors who help you scale as well as invest, come to us.

We introduce investors whose mandate includes operating support, co-investment and follow-on capital, and we confirm that fit before either side hears the other's name.

03

If you need Gulf capital, come to us.

We hold relationships with sovereign funds and family offices in the region and prepare managers for how those investors engage.
