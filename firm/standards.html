Our standards

What every client and every investor can expect from us, on every mandate.

Learn more about our principles

Confidentiality

Live mandates are never named. When asked who we work with, we give sector and size only, and what a client sends us stays with us.

Equity focus

We advise on equity raises and on mergers and acquisitions. Debt, credit and lending mandates are outside our practice.

Engagements in writing

We act for the client that engages us. Scope and terms are confirmed in writing before any work begins.

Decisions with reasons

Our answer follows senior review. When it is no, we say so and give the reasons.

What we publish

Every figure on this website carries a public source, so that any reader can check it.
