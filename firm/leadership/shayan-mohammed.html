Leadership

Shayan Mohammed

Managing Director

Shayan Mohammed is Managing Director of Inaya Advisory. He leads the firm's investor coverage across North America, Europe and the Gulf, and the growth of the firm itself.

Shayan decides which investors each mandate reaches, and in what order. Once a raise is positioned it goes first to the investors we work with, in the sequence most likely to bring early commitments.

The order matters because allocators look at who has already committed. A raise that reaches the right investor first gives every later conversation something to point to.
