Leadership

Anas Ansar

Managing Partner

Anas Ansar is Managing Partner of Inaya Advisory. He runs our mandates day to day, from the first review of a raise to its final close, and signs each engagement for the firm.

Anas leads origination and leads senior review with David Hunter. Once a mandate is accepted he directs the deck, the data room and the terms review, then runs the raise in market until the capital is committed.

What Anas owns

Origination

Leads the development of new mandates with fund managers, sponsors and founders raising equity.

Senior review

Reviews every new situation with David Hunter. The principal has our answer, with the reasons for it, once that review is complete.

Positioning and materials

Directs the deck, the data room and the terms review before any investor sees the raise.

Execution

Runs the raise in market, through investor meetings and diligence questions to each close.

Engagement

Sets the scope of each engagement in writing and signs it on the firm's behalf.
