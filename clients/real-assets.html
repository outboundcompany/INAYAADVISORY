Real Assets Sponsors

We advise sponsors raising equity for real estate and infrastructure, through funds, programmes and individual projects, and prepare each raise asset by asset.

440 of 951

Source: SEC EDGAR Form D filings, twelve months to July 2026. Inaya Advisory analysis, published in Open private raises. Open private offerings of $50 million or more.

Real assets sponsors

An allocator that covers real estate sees many of these raises at once, and reads each one asset by asset.

Much of this equity comes from pension funds, insurers and endowments investing on behalf of their members. We prepare every raise for that reader.

Record by asset

Deal-level results for each asset, with realised results kept apart from unrealised and each one tied to the team that delivered it.

Pipeline

The assets the capital will go into first, how far along each one is and what its business plan requires.

Alignment

The sponsor's own commitment and terms, compared with the market for the strategy and tested against the plan.

When managers come to us

The situations that bring a real assets sponsor to us.

If you need an anchor investor, come to us.

The first commitment decides how every investor after it reads the raise. We take the raise to the investors most likely to anchor it, in a set order, before the market has formed a view.

If a raise is stalling, come to us.

A raise open for months usually needs a change in who it is speaking to and what they are shown. We reposition the materials and take it back out to a different order of investors.

If you need Gulf capital, come to us.

We hold relationships with sovereign funds and family offices in the region and prepare managers for how those investors engage.
