Confidentiality

An email from us is intended only for the person or organisation it is addressed to, and it may contain confidential information. If you have received it in error, please tell the sender by replying to it, then delete it. Please do not copy it, use it or pass it on.

No offer and no advice

Nothing in an email from us is an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security. It is not a recommendation, and it is not investment, legal or tax advice.

Securities are offered through Finalis Securities LLC (CRD# 305908), Member FINRA/SIPC. Inaya Advisory is not a registered broker-dealer. Inaya Advisory and Finalis Securities LLC are separate, unaffiliated entities. See our regulatory disclosures.

No agreement by email

An email does not create an advisory, agency or fiduciary relationship, or any other agreement. An engagement exists only once both parties have signed a written agreement setting out its terms.

Information and figures

Information in an email, including figures about markets, is provided for information only and may change without notice. Figures describing the prior experience of members of our team relate to their earlier roles and are not transactions of Inaya Advisory.

Sending information by email

Email sent over the internet may not be secure. It can be intercepted, delayed or changed before it arrives. Please keep this in mind before you send us sensitive information by email.

Personal information

We handle personal information in emails in line with our Privacy Policy. Questions about this notice can be sent to capital@inayaadvisory.com.
