How securities activity is conducted

Securities are offered through Finalis Securities LLC, an SEC-registered broker-dealer and member of FINRA and SIPC (CRD No. 305908). Inaya Advisory is not a registered broker-dealer. Inaya Advisory and Finalis Securities LLC are separate, unaffiliated entities. Finalis's Office of Supervisory Jurisdiction is at 450 Lexington Ave, New York, NY 10017, telephone 800-962-0418. Its Form CRS describes its services and how it is paid. Which entity acts in a given engagement is set out in the engagement letter.

Corporate structure

Inaya Advisory is the trading name of Inaya LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company formed on 3 June 2026 under filing ID 2026-001995889. Its registered office is 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, the address of its registered agent. Finalis Securities LLC is not part of this structure.

Engagement and fees

Each mandate is governed by a written engagement letter agreed before work begins, and the fees for that mandate are set out there. We are paid an engagement fee, credited in full against a success fee. The success fee applies only to capital committed by investors we introduce, and remains payable for twelve months after a mandate ends on those investors. The engagement fee is not refundable. On a securities transaction, fees are paid as the engagement letter and the Finalis agreement set out. These ranges are indicative.

Outcomes

Whether capital is committed is decided by investors after their own diligence and approval, and depends on market conditions. No outcome is guaranteed, and securities rules bar any promise of one.

Investor relationships

References to investor relationships describe relationships our team has built over their careers. Figures describing the prior experience of our team relate to their earlier roles and are not transactions of Inaya Advisory.

Senior advisors

Senior advisors are independent professionals who contribute advisory input, industry experience and relationship access to engagements.

No offer or solicitation

No securities offering is made through this website. Nothing on it is an offer to sell or a solicitation of an offer to buy any security. Any offer is made only to eligible investors, through definitive offering documents, and in compliance with applicable securities laws.

Investment risk

Private placements carry a high degree of risk. They may be illiquid and speculative, may be restricted on transfer, and can result in the loss of all of the capital invested.

Insights and market data

Articles under Insights are general information about private markets. They are not investment research and do not recommend any security, fund or transaction. Where we publish a figure, we date it and name its source. Third-party data comes from sources we believe reliable but have not independently verified.

Enquiries and confidentiality

Sending an enquiry does not create an engagement. Please send a general description first, and agree confidentiality terms with us before sharing confidential documents or material non-public information. We sign a non-disclosure agreement before receiving non-public information.

Fraud notice

Our website is inayaadvisory.com. For business development we also send email from domains that use variations of the Inaya Advisory name. Our outreach goes to companies and fund managers raising capital. We never ask you to send money to a personal account, pay a fee to release funds, share a password or code, or buy cryptocurrency. Any message that does is not from us. If in doubt, email capital@inayaadvisory.com before acting.

No professional advice

Nothing on this site is legal, tax, accounting or investment advice. Please consult your own advisors.

Terms of use

Use of this site is governed by our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. Where anything on this site differs from an engagement letter or transaction documents, those documents govern.
